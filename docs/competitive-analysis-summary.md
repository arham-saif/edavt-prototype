# Competitive Analysis Summary

EDAVT does not claim that education analytics is a new category. Its differentiation is the combination of capabilities in one open, standards-based platform.

## Differentiators

- **Open standards first:** Ed-Fi, OneRoster, LTI, xAPI, and QTI integration paths.
- **Explainable predictive analytics:** SHAP-style explanations for individual risk scores.
- **Qualitative signal support:** design path for counselor notes, teacher comments, and student self-assessments, subject to privacy constraints.
- **Open-source Community Edition:** a path for under-resourced districts that cannot afford enterprise analytics platforms.
- **Cloud-agnostic deployment:** managed SaaS, private cloud, and self-hosted options.
- **Privacy and audit workflow:** data minimization, pseudonymization, role scoping, and event logging.

## Positioning

Commercial systems such as SIS analytics modules, enterprise BI dashboards, and student success platforms can provide useful reporting. EDAVT is positioned as a complementary and alternative approach for districts that need lower-cost, standards-native, transparent, and privacy-accountable analytics.

## Next Validation Work

The strongest next validation work would be:

- feedback from district technology or data leaders;
- pilot discovery conversations;
- benchmark validation against public or synthetic datasets;
- third-party technical review;
- documented open-source packaging.
